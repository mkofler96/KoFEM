# Simple words and short sentences

## Complex → simpler words

Use the simplest word that still carries the exact meaning. Context decides: *complete the form* beats *fill out the form* for readers whose first language is not English, even though *fill out* is more casual.

## Sentence length

One concept, thought, or idea per sentence. Count the verbs — more than two actions means split it.

Before (six verbs, one sentence):
> Use the TT Quicklinks on the team wiki to create the tickets, which will be assigned to the ticket queue, which associates will open and give priority to those with the highest severity working from the oldest ticket.

After:
> To create a ticket, use one of the Quicklinks on the team wiki. We prioritize tickets by severity (highest first), beginning with the oldest.

Do not split when both ideas are simple and belong together:
> Create a new ticket and, on the Communications tab, type your responses to the questions.

Long sentences are not banned. Split when splitting helps the reader follow the order of events or see who is responsible for what.
