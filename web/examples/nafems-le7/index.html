NAFEMS LE7: cylinder with hemispherical cap

A thin cylinder closed by a hemispherical cap, under internal pressure. Away from the junction the cylinder carries the membrane stresses pr/t around and pr/2t along it, and the hemisphere pr/2t in every direction; the two would expand by different amounts, so the junction bends. NAFEMS reads the axial stress on the outer surface just below the junction, σzz=25.9 MPa at D.

Analytical solution

Away from the junction both parts carry the pressure as membranes:

cylinder: σθθ=prt,σzz=pr2t;sphere: σ=pr2t.

The cylinder's hoop strain is larger than the sphere's, so the two would separate at the junction; bending closes the gap and decays over a length of about rt. At D, inside that length, the outer surface carries the membrane stress plus that bending.

A quarter of the vessel, its cut meridians held by symmetry, imported as a 25 mm solid and marked Shell: the app collapses the cylinder and the cap to one welded mid-surface. The bottom edge F is held axially only. The pressure of 1 MPa acts on the outer face, pulling outward, and the outer surface is the shell's top surface, where the probe reads σzz at D.

Discussion

KoFEM reads +0.50 % from the NAFEMS target. The convergence study shows the bending at the junction resolved once a few elements span its decay length.

Reproduce

In the app: open the model, with its supports, loads and settings.

Or build it yourself, as this page's number was built:

  1. Import nafems-le7.step.
  2. Set the body to Shell. Meshing measures the 25 mm wall and replaces it by its mid-surface; the faces through the wall become the shell's edges, which you pick as Edges.
  3. Mesh with a 25 mm maximum element size, linear elements.
  4. Material Steel: E = 210000 MPa, ν = 0.3.
  5. Support “Bottom edge, axial”: Uz = 0 on the edge through (707.107, 707.107, 0).
  6. Support “Symmetry y = 0”: Uy, Rx, Rz = 0 on the edge through (1000, 0, 750).
  7. Support “Symmetry x = 0”: Ux, Ry, Rz = 0 on the edge through (6.12323e-14, 1000, 750).
  8. Load “Internal pressure”: pressure −1 MPa on the edges through (715.946, 715.946, 750), (506.25, 506.25, 2215.95).
  9. Run the static study. The Results panel compares axial stress at d with NAFEMS LE7 target.
