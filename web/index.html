KoFEM

Finite element analysis in your browser.

Import a STEP or IGES part, apply loads and supports, and get displacements and stresses — or let topology optimization find a lighter shape.

  • Nothing to install. No installer, no sign-up.
  • Computed in your browser. The Netgen mesher and MFEM solver run locally as WebAssembly, not on a server.
  • Your CAD stays on your machine. Files, meshes and results are never uploaded; opt-in analytics never see them (privacy notice).
  • Open source. AGPL-3.0, code on GitHub.
